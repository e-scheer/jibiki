# こ → cobra

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

INPUT CONTRACT
Image 1 is the exact immutable 「こ」 and the complete character layer. Image 2 is
style reference only. Preserve both white strokes, their endpoints, spacing,
positions and proportions. Generate no additional character-layer pixels.

TARGET
Transform exact 「こ」 into one upright cobra with its hood expanded, facing right.
No basket, charmer, scenery, second snake or separate complete colored cobra.

STROKE OWNERSHIP
1. The upper white horizontal stroke IS the complete upper edge of the cobra's
   flattened head and expanded hood. Its right endpoint IS the snout. Add one small
   eye and a forked tongue touching that endpoint. Add only two broken hood-side
   fragments descending from the stroke's endpoints; no colored top edge.
2. The lower white stroke's upward-curving left end IS the cobra's raised neck. Its
   long lower horizontal portion IS the load-bearing underside of the coiled body and
   tail. Add only a broken scale/hood fragment attached to the neck and one incomplete
   upper coil contour terminating twice on the white lower stroke. Never draw a
   complete colored neck, coil or tail around it.
3. A small illustration-color hood marking may sit between the two white strokes, but
   it must remain an isolated marking rather than a body fill.

CONTACTS
Eye touches the upper stroke; tongue begins at its right endpoint; both hood sides
terminate on the upper stroke and raised white neck; upper coil fragment terminates
twice on the lower white body stroke; a tiny tail-tip fragment touches its far-right
endpoint. Nothing floats nearby.

EXPRESSIVITY BUDGET
Make the cobra theatrical, alert and slightly menacing rather than icon-like. Give it
one sharply focused eye with an angled brow fragment, two tiny ILLUSTRATION-COLOR fang fragments, a long
forked tongue curling forward, strongly flared asymmetric hood sides, three attached
scale markings, and three short tension/motion marks around the raised head. Exaggerate
the lifted neck and the spring tension of the coil. These remain illustration-layer
fragments and may not close a complete colored snake silhouette.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide 「こ」: the cobra loses its head roof, neck and entire underside, leaving only
disconnected eye, tongue, hood sides, marking and coil fragment. A complete colored
snake silhouette is failure.

LAYERS
Three aligned full-canvas layers: BACKGROUND, EXACT_CHARACTER_こ, ILLUSTRATION.
Preview hues are placeholders; the app recolors isolated layers by theme.

STYLE
Portrait 4:5, one centered expressive mid-century educational editorial illustration,
bold rounded screen-print fragments, lively asymmetry, worked but uncluttered. Do not
render a bare minimalist icon. No text, extra glyph, UI, logo, watermark or secondary subject.
```
