# え → pêcher

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

INPUT CONTRACT
Image 1 is the exact immutable 「え」 and the complete character layer. Image 2 is
style reference only. Preserve every white stroke, endpoint, angle, junction, gap,
position and proportion. Generate no additional character-layer pixels.

TARGET
Transform exact 「え」 into one fishing action: a float at the water surface, a taut
line descending underwater, and one fish biting the hook. No fisherman, boat, shore,
second fish or complete colored fishing rig.

STROKE OWNERSHIP
1. The detached short upper white stroke IS the fishing float. Add only two small
   illustration-color end caps and one tiny connector descending from its midpoint.
   Never draw another float around it.
2. The long upper white stroke of the main form IS the water surface and the rigid
   top section of the fishing rig. Add only two short ripple fragments that terminate
   on it. Do not draw a second continuous waterline.
3. The long white diagonal descending down-left IS the taut fishing line below the
   surface. Add no parallel colored line. One small colored knot may wrap its upper
   junction.
4. The white curve rising from the bottom center and turning right IS the bent hook
   under tension; its final lower-right horizontal segment IS the hook shank. Add only
   one tiny colored barb at its far-right endpoint.
5. Add one incomplete fish in illustration ink biting that exact white endpoint: a
   small head/mouth wraps the hook endpoint, while two broken body contours and a tail
   stop before forming a closed fish silhouette. The white hook must remain visible.

CONTACTS
Float connector touches the upper main junction; ripples touch the white waterline;
knot wraps the line junction; barb touches the far-right hook endpoint; fish mouth
wraps that endpoint; two fish fragments terminate near the white hook without
covering it.

EXPRESSIVITY BUDGET
Show a dramatic bite rather than a static diagram. Tilt the float, bend its connector,
make the white line feel visibly taut, and give the incomplete fish a startled eye,
open gripping mouth and strongly flicked tail. Add three splash droplets, two bubbles,
three energetic ripples and two short line-vibration marks. Let some droplets and the
fish mouth overlap the white hook locally. Keep all additions fragmentary: no closed
colored fish, complete water scene or duplicate fishing line.

Put most expressive density in the eye, mouth, bubbles, splashes, ripples and vibration
marks. The fish itself must remain open line fragments: one mouth arc, one eye, one
short back arc, one short belly arc and two separated tail triangles. Keep large gaps
between mouth, back, belly and tail. Do not use a filled body mass and do not connect
these fragments into a surviving fish silhouette.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide 「え」: float, water surface, line and hook disappear, and the fish collapses into
disconnected mouth/body/tail fragments. A complete colored fishing rod, line, hook or
fish is failure.

LAYERS
Three aligned full-canvas layers: BACKGROUND, EXACT_CHARACTER_え, ILLUSTRATION.
Preview hues are placeholders; the app recolors isolated layers by theme.

STYLE
Portrait 4:5, one centered expressive mid-century educational editorial illustration,
playful rounded screen-print shapes, visible tension, splashes and asymmetry, worked
but uncluttered. Do not render a bare minimalist diagram. No text, extra glyph, UI,
logo, watermark, boat, fisherman or secondary subject.
```
