# Test prompt v4 — あ / umbrella shelter

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese kana visual mnemonic

PRIMARY REQUEST
Create one minimal mnemonic pictogram from the exact hiragana 「あ」 and an umbrella
sheltering a person from rain. The character must be the load-bearing skeleton of
the illustration, not typography placed over a finished picture.

INPUT ROLES
- Exact-glyph reference: use its 「あ」 topology and stroke placement as the immutable
  character blueprint.
- Style reference: use only its flat educational mnemonic style, sparse composition,
  three-color palette, and clever foreground/background overlaps. Ignore all UI and
  all text in the style reference.

MANDATORY STROKE-BINDING BLUEPRINT
1. The short upper HORIZONTAL stroke of 「あ」 IS the rigid lower rim / roof edge of
   the umbrella canopy. The colored canopy must be an incomplete curved cap built
   ABOVE that exact white stroke and must terminate at its two endpoints. Do not draw
   any colored lower rim, horizontal canopy band, or second roof edge.
2. The near-vertical stroke crossing that horizontal stroke IS the umbrella shaft.
   Place the grip and the hand directly ON this exact white stroke. Do not draw a
   parallel colored shaft and do not replace the white stroke with a colored one.
3. The large lower-left loop of 「あ」 IS the hunched shoulder and bent arm of the
   person sheltering below the umbrella. Add only a tiny colored head, hand and broken
   clothing contours that connect to it; never draw a complete colored torso or arm.
4. The sweeping lower-right curve of 「あ」 IS the other bent arm and the wind-blown
   edge of the raincoat. Add only short colored joints and motion/rain marks so this
   function is immediately readable.

CONSTRUCTION ORDER
First reproduce one exact, large, upright 「あ」. Before drawing anything else,
identify the four stroke groups above. Then construct only the missing fragments of
the umbrella and sheltered person around those strokes. Every colored contour must
start or stop at a specified kana stroke or be visibly interrupted by it.

DEPTH
- Behind 「あ」: only the upper curved canopy surface and sparse rain strokes.
- Exact 「あ」: one unmodified solid white glyph.
- In front of 「あ」: only the hand gripping the white shaft, a small head, tiny joint
  connectors, and a few broken clothing contours.

CRITICAL FAILURE CONDITIONS
- A complete colored umbrella exists without 「あ」.
- A colored shaft runs beside or over the exact white vertical stroke.
- A colored horizontal canopy edge duplicates the white horizontal stroke.
- The canopy merely floats above the kana without terminating on the horizontal
  stroke endpoints.
- The lower loops remain unused typography instead of the person's body/arms.
- Any stroke of 「あ」 is moved, omitted, bent, extended, redrawn, or recolored.

REMOVAL TEST
If every white pixel is hidden, the remaining colored marks must look like broken,
disconnected fragments: a canopy missing its structural lower rim, a hand holding no
shaft, and clothing fragments missing their arms and body. If a complete generic
umbrella or person remains, reject the image and rebuild it.

STYLE AND COLOR
Portrait 4:5, centered, large pictogram, generous empty margins. Flat mid-century
educational vector illustration, bold rounded shapes, playful and extremely sparse.
Exactly three flat colors: warm yellow background, solid white 「あ」, one muted
brick-red color for every illustration mark. No gradients, shadows, texture, black,
secondary accents, transparency effects, or 3D.

NO TEXT
The single 「あ」 is the only written glyph. No word, caption, Latin letter, number,
extra Japanese character, UI, border, logo, signature, or watermark.
```
