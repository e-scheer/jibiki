# と → taureau

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

INPUT CONTRACT
Image 1 is the exact immutable 「と」 and the complete character layer. Image 2 is
style reference only. Preserve every stroke, branch, endpoint, curve, position and
proportion. Generate no additional character-layer pixels.

TARGET
Transform the exact 「と」 into the load-bearing skeleton of one powerful bull
charging toward the left, seen from the side. No rider, matador, arena or second animal.

STROKE OWNERSHIP
1. The tall white branch rising at upper left IS the near horn.
2. The white diagonal branch rising toward the upper right IS the far horn. Add only
   tiny illustration-color bases wrapping the two horn roots; never draw other horns.
3. The junction between the two branches IS the bull's forehead and skull hinge. Add
   one small eye, one ear and broken face contours attached directly to this junction.
4. The large white curve sweeping down and around the left IS the lowered muzzle,
   throat and front chest contour. Add nostril and a few broken hair/skin contours,
   but never surround it with a complete colored head.
5. The long white lower stroke extending right IS the bull's load-bearing belly line
   toward the hindquarters. Add one broken upper-back contour, a rear-haunch fragment,
   four short leg fragments and a tail, all terminating on or visually interrupted by
   this white belly structure. Do not draw a closed colored body silhouette.

CONTACTS
Horn bases wrap exact branch roots; eye/ear touch the skull junction; face fragments
terminate on the large white curve; shoulder and leg joints touch the white chest or
belly line; rear haunch touches the far-right endpoint; tail touches the haunch.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide 「と」: horns, face, chest and belly disappear, leaving disconnected eye, ear,
back, legs, haunch and tail fragments. A complete colored bull is failure.

LAYERS
Three aligned full-canvas layers: BACKGROUND, EXACT_CHARACTER_と, ILLUSTRATION.
Preview hues are placeholders; the app recolors isolated layers by theme.

STYLE
Portrait 4:5, one centered energetic mid-century educational pictogram, bold rounded
screen-print fragments and minimal motion marks. No text, extra glyph, UI, logo,
watermark, arena or secondary subject.
```
