# か → katana

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

INPUT CONTRACT
Image 1 is the exact immutable 「か」 and the complete character layer. Image 2 is
style reference only. Preserve every character stroke, endpoint, crossing, gap,
position and proportion. Generate no additional character-layer pixels.

TARGET
Transform the exact 「か」 into one katana partly drawn from its scabbard. No samurai,
hands, scenery or second weapon. Drawing fragments must depend on the character.

STROKE OWNERSHIP
1. The short upper portion of the main left diagonal stroke IS the wrapped katana
   handle. Add only short illustration-color wrapping bands crossing it.
2. The short white horizontal crossbar IS the entire sword guard. Add one tiny hub
   around its crossing, but no second guard.
3. The long white diagonal continuing down-left below the guard IS the exposed curved
   blade. Add only a tiny point cap at its lower endpoint and one short shine/motion
   fragment in illustration ink. Never draw a parallel blade edge.
4. The large white curve on the center-right IS the scabbard. Add only two incomplete
   outer lacquer contours whose endpoints terminate on that curve, plus a small mouth
   fitting at its upper attachment and a tip fitting at its lower hook. No complete
   colored scabbard may surround it.
5. The detached white curved stroke at far right IS the hanging scabbard cord. Add one
   small illustration-color knot touching its upper endpoint and two tiny tassel
   fragments at its lower endpoint. Never duplicate the cord.

CONTACTS
Handle bands cross the white handle; hub wraps the guard crossing; scabbard mouth
touches the guard area; scabbard contour terminates twice on the large white curve;
knot and tassels touch the detached white cord endpoints.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide 「か」: only disconnected handle bands, fittings, contour fragments, knot and
tassels remain. A complete sword, blade or scabbard in illustration ink is failure.

LAYERS
Three aligned full-canvas layers: BACKGROUND, EXACT_CHARACTER_か, ILLUSTRATION.
Preview colors only need strong separation; the app recolors layers by theme.

STYLE
Portrait 4:5, one centered sparse mid-century educational pictogram, bold rounded
screen-print shapes. No text, extra glyph, UI, logo, watermark or second subject.
```
