# み → mitraillette

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

INPUT CONTRACT
Image 1 is the exact immutable 「み」 and the complete character layer. Image 2 is
style reference only. Preserve every stroke, endpoint, crossing, opening, position
and proportion. Generate no additional character-layer pixels.

TARGET
Transform the exact 「み」 into one completely non-functional, friendly 1960s cartoon
toy prop shaped like an imaginary compact pop-gun, used only as a visual association
for the French word “mitraillette”. No realism, firing, bullets, ammunition, person,
conflict, military scene, injury, threatening pose or second object.

STROKE OWNERSHIP
1. The short white upper-left stroke IS the upper edge of the shoulder stock. Add only
   a broken illustration-color lower stock contour terminating on its two endpoints.
2. The long white diagonal descending from the upper center IS the lower stock and
   rear receiver support. Never draw a parallel complete stock.
3. The large white loop at lower left IS the trigger guard. Add one tiny curved
   trigger inside the loop in illustration ink, but no second trigger-guard outline.
4. The long white stroke crossing the center and extending right IS the toy casing's
   load-bearing axis and barrel-like tube. Add only incomplete casing panels that stop
   on it, plus a large rounded plugged cap touching its far-right endpoint so it is
   visibly incapable of firing.
5. The short white upright above the right crossing IS a decorative toy sight.
6. The long white curve descending on the right IS a curved toy battery pack. Add two
   short outer fragments that terminate on it and a tiny attachment collar at the
   crossing. Never draw a complete colored pack around it.

CONTACTS
Stock contour touches both ends of the upper-left white stroke; trigger sits inside
the white loop; receiver fragments terminate on the central white axis; muzzle cap
touches its right endpoint; magazine collar wraps the crossing; magazine fragments
terminate on the right white curve.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide 「み」: stock, toy casing, tube, trigger guard, sight and battery pack all lose
their essential structure. A complete colored toy silhouette is failure.

LAYERS
Three aligned full-canvas layers: BACKGROUND, EXACT_CHARACTER_み, ILLUSTRATION.
Preview hues are placeholders; only clean layer separation matters.

STYLE
Portrait 4:5, one centered cheerful children's 1960s educational screen-print
pictogram, rounded and obviously non-realistic. No text, labels, extra glyph, person,
violence, ammunition, muzzle flash, realistic weapon detail, UI, logo or watermark.
```
