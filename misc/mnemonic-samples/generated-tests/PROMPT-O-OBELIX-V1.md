# Test prompt — お / Obélix

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

PRIMARY REQUEST
Transform one exact, large, upright hiragana 「お」 into the load-bearing body of
Obelix from Asterix. He must be immediately recognizable from his huge round belly,
tiny head, winged helmet, two braids, belt and small legs, but he must not exist as a
complete colored character behind or beside the kana.

INPUT ROLES
- Exact-glyph reference: immutable structural blueprint for 「お」.
- Style reference: flat educational mnemonic style and sparse three-color
  composition only. Ignore every UI element and every piece of text.

MANDATORY STROKE-BINDING BLUEPRINT
1. The enormous lower-right white loop of 「お」 IS Obelix's huge round belly. Add only
   a few short brick-red broken clothing contours at its endpoints. Never draw a
   separate colored belly outline.
2. The lower-left white loop IS one bent leg and rounded boot. Add only a tiny colored
   ankle joint or sole fragment. Never draw a complete colored leg or boot.
3. The tall central white stroke IS the other leg and the vertical load-bearing axis
   of his torso. Build the tiny head and shoulders around its upper endpoint without
   covering, extending or replacing it.
4. The short upper horizontal white stroke IS Obelix's belt across his body. Add one
   small brick-red oval buckle directly around its central crossing, but no second
   colored belt line.
5. The detached diagonal white stroke at upper right IS one large wing of Obelix's
   helmet. The brick-red helmet dome must terminate at its inner endpoint. Add only
   one small opposite wing in brick-red and two minimal braids, so the white detached
   stroke is indispensable to the helmet silhouette.

CONSTRUCTION RULE
First lock the exact white 「お」. Then draw only the missing brick-red fragments of
Obelix around it: tiny face, broken helmet dome, one opposite wing, two braids, hands,
buckle, and minimal joints. Every important red fragment must attach to a named white
stroke. The white kana must remain dominant and perfectly readable.

DEPTH
- Behind the glyph: at most tiny hair/braid fragments and incomplete clothing fills.
- Exact glyph: one unmodified solid-white 「お」.
- In front: tiny face details, hands gripping the white structure, belt buckle and
  short joint connectors only.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide all white pixels. What remains must collapse into disconnected fragments: a
helmet missing one wing and its support, a buckle without a belt, clothing without a
belly, and joints without legs. If a complete Obelix silhouette remains in brick red,
reject and rebuild.

FAILURE CONDITIONS
- Obelix is drawn completely behind, in front of, beside, or sitting on typography.
- The white lower-right loop is merely inside a separately drawn colored belly.
- A colored belt duplicates the white horizontal stroke.
- The detached white stroke is not physically joined to the helmet.
- Any white stroke is moved, omitted, bent, extended, redrawn or recolored.

STYLE AND COLOR
Portrait 4:5, one centered large pictogram, generous empty margins. Cheerful
mid-century educational vector / screen-print illustration, rounded and extremely
sparse. Exactly three hard-edged flat solid colors: warm yellow background, solid
white 「お」, and one muted brick-red ink for all additions. Absolutely no gradients,
lighting, glow, shadows, texture, transparency effects, black, or secondary accent.

NO TEXT
The single 「お」 is the only written glyph. No word, name, caption, Latin letter,
number, extra Japanese character, UI, border, logo, signature, or watermark.
```
