Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Japanese hiragana visual mnemonic

PRIMARY REQUEST
Use one exact solid-white hiragana 「お」 as the indispensable body skeleton of a
compact, heavy Obelix carrying a menhir. Do not place a finished character on top of
the glyph. Add only disconnected brick-red anatomy and costume fragments.

ABSOLUTE WHITE-PIXEL RULE
The exact supplied 「お」 is the entire white layer. Create no other white pixel or
white shape anywhere: no white helmet wing, eye, clothing, highlight, tooth, hand,

1. The huge lower-right white loop IS Obelix's enormous belly and rounded right

OBELIX IDENTITY FRAGMENTS

dome, two small red helmet wings, a large round nose, moustache, two short braids and
tiny eyes cut from the yellow background. Add one red hand on the belt and one hand

red silhouette.

REMOVAL TEST
Hide the white 「お」. The red remainder must be unusable fragments: a head with no
torso, buckle with no belt, soles with no legs, and a hand gripping no menhir. If a
complete Obelix or complete menhir remains, reject and rebuild.

- Any extra white mark appears outside the exact supplied 「お」.
- A long white neck or generic stick figure replaces compact Obelix proportions.
- The right loop is treated as empty typography instead of belly/trousers.
- A complete colored character is drawn behind or around the kana.

STYLE AND COLOR
Portrait 4:5. Compact figure centered on the exact glyph. Flat 1960s educational
screen-print mnemonic, playful, rounded, extremely sparse. Exactly three flat solid
colors: warm yellow background, the original solid-white 「お」 only, and one muted
brick-red for every added mark. No gradient, glow, shadow, texture, black, secondary
accent or 3D.

NO TEXT
Only the single 「お」. No name, caption, Latin letter, number, other Japanese glyph,
UI, logo, signature or watermark.
